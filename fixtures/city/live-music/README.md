# Live music in the city

Set the system output (or your music app's output) to **BlackHole 2ch**, at 48 kHz. Connect your AirPods, open `run-workbench-live-music.command`, grant Terminal microphone access on first launch, and press **Play**. macOS treats loopback capture as microphone input. The speaker starts off. WASD walks, drag looks, and Shift sprints, as in the street demo.

One 4 m stereo image sits 8 m up at ENU `[493.8,473.0,8.0]`, just outside the northwest facade corner of building `synth/4/5/2`. The pair is omnidirectional; pose forward `[0.987496,-0.157637,0]` orients the 4 m axis across the listener's view. Authored L is the north/east endpoint `[494.115,474.975,8]` (listener-left at start), and R the south/west endpoint `[493.485,471.025,8]` (listener-right). Start is `[426.02,483.82,1.5]`, 68.95 m from the image center with mesh-checked line of sight. Walk east along the street, then south near x=530 to put that building between you and the image. HRTF direct, air absorption, validated routed pathing, eight-bounce reflections, and the existing output protection remain active.

The launcher uses the retained `megablock-seed1/megablock.fightbox` package and `successor-path1500-vis40.baked`. Its serialized radius-8 m probe spheres at `[492.55,476.95,1.5]` and `[492.55,468.95,1.5]` cover the speaker at distances 7.708 m and 7.760 m; three spheres cover the listener start. The adjacent mesh facade spans x=494.3..525.7, y=437.8..472.2, z=0..38.4 m; the speaker is outside it in the open street corridor, rather than inside a building.

Calibration: nominal mastered music −14 dBFS RMS + drive −5 dB = −19 dBFS at 1 m (125 dB SPL). Each stereo direct plane receives half the source drive. The shared indirect send is the mean `0.5 × (L + R)`, so correlated stereo has the same level as the A1 mono feed. For uncorrelated synthetic stereo, compare against its mono-fold render and require the distant-start level to stay within 1 dB. At the 68.95 m center distance, `−19 − 20 log10(68.95) + default monitor 30` is **−25.77 dBFS RMS** free-field at start before HRTF, air and returns; the limiter stays on. This estimates scene PCM, not headphone SPL. Actual music level varies; the 0 dB monitor capture is 30 dB quieter.

Test-only: `--live-input-wav /absolute/existing/music.wav` feeds the same ring in real time without opening an input device; combine with headless replay, explicit output `--device "BlackHole 2ch"`, and an external capture root. Normal live input requires a different output device to prevent feedback.

The two planes share the center's propagation clock and acoustic simulation. Routed and reflected sound use the mean; phase-opposed content can cancel in that send. At the start yaw of 99.07°, L is at −1.669° and R at +1.669° (right positive). Compare each tone's ear balance against the matching mono capture: Steam's centered HRTF is asymmetric, so absolute 440 Hz left-ear dominance is not guaranteed at this distance.
