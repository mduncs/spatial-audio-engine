# Recorded building-height regression inputs

Small extracts of the 2026-10-02 Loop 350 m and Ravenswood Overpass responses.
© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. The response timestamps remain in each file.

- `landmark-outlines.json`: Willis Tower (`way/380868216`) and Chase Tower
  (`way/230613007`), with their referenced nodes.
- `willis-parts.json`: the 442 m roof (`way/137162296`) and two 527 m antenna
  footprints (`way/233918141`, `way/233918220`), plus the four Chase parts
  (`way/284778256`–`way/284778259`), with referenced nodes.
- `neighborhood-house.json`: the two-level house at 1620 W Sunnyside Avenue
  (`way/209926304`), measured at 9.4 m in the cached Cook County LiDAR.
- `neighborhood-parts.json`: the recorded empty parts response.

These are map data extracts, not SDK or audio assets.
